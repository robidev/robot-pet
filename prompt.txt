please have a look at \home\user\robot-pet, especially the todo.txt, and vacuum-api and face-api. I want to make a robot pet with these ingredients;

The robot-vacuum is the mobile robot platform. it can navigate the room, already includes SLAM, obstacle avoidance, cliff avoidance, AStar and DStar path planning, home&docking routine, so we only care about the high-level functions like goto, drive, stop and dock. All needed functions are exposed via vacuum-api, besides the timestamped pose of the robot, which is exposed via playerc-client(with a small caveat regarding the timestamp for player being since boot, not UTC)

The face is mounted with a pan+tilt servo on the robot (on top of the lidar to be precise). servos are controlled by the face. it has a camera that can send video/stills. It can stream audio from a microphone over udp with 16khz, 16-bit PCM. it has a 'face' in the form of a robotic-eye(aperture) that can convey some sense of a robotic-intelligence. A pir for movement-detection. It will send events timestamped, and with pose-info, as well as some queries via the web-api will contain pose and timestamps. Also is the camera setup for face detection and recognition, and the face can remember 7 faces. A face is stored by triggering the function in the web-api, when a face is present, and then will be coupled to a face-ID. Faces will also be tracked by the servos when in tracking mode (thus eye contact is maintained). 

The audio is received on udp port 5000, and converted via whisper.cpp, using a VAD to text (currently it is only a command-line app). This needs to be 'plugged' into a customizable LLM. lets for now just send it to claude-cli(if possible), maybe ollama later.(account for this)

The LLM should be able to have an awareness of its postioning inside a robot, the abilities it has(move, see, hear, show emotions) a personality, and some background info, backstory and motivation,

responses from the LLM may be converted from text to speech using piper-tts, and streamed to the robot-vacuum, as it has a set-up sound system.
Besides text responses, also commands can be send to the face, or robot-vacuum platform

Suggest for each step the recommended model/effort, and cluster where possible, so we can run workloads without switching too much.
